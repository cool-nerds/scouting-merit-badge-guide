01 / Get registered

From interested parent to approved counselor

Adults of good character age 18 or older can apply. Subject expertise may come from work, education, formal training or sustained practical experience. A professional license is not a blanket requirement for every badge. Describe competence for the entire badge and arrange qualified specialists wherever policy requires them.

  1. Choose your badges. Review the current requirements and counselor notes. Identify your experience, gaps and any instructor or activity-supervisor credentials.
  2. Prepare your account and training. Use your existing My.Scouting account and member ID. Complete current Safeguarding Youth training. Take Merit Badge Counselor Position-Specific Training and confirm council expectations.
  3. Follow the local approval route below. Complete the official council form, required screening and the registration steps applicable to you. This supplement helps you explain your qualifications.
  4. Wait for the council decision. Confirm your counselor registration and the specific approved badges. A unit signature, sent email or completed training course does not by itself approve a counselor.
  5. Verify and maintain your record. Check your approved counselor position and badges in Scoutbook; ask council to correct missing records. Maintain annual registration, current training and activity qualifications. Confirm any additional badges before counseling them.

Spirit of Adventure’s published application route

Already a registered leader in this council? Have another unit Key 3 member approve the MBC form; self-approval is not allowed. Email the completed form to membership@scoutingboston.org.

Not currently a registered leader in this council? Obtain district advancement chair approval and email the form to that chair and council membership. Complete SYT, the council CORI JotForm and the online adult application; council sends the application link after receiving the MBC form.

The published MBC-only fee is $25 plus 3% administration. Confirm your existing registration and any payment due with membership.

02 / Know the distinction

“I have the skills. Can I counsel this badge?”

Scuba Diving

“I am PADI certified.”

Specify your actual rating: Open Water Diver, Divemaster and Instructor are different qualifications. A council-approved counselor does not have to be a diving instructor, but scuba instruction and the Open Water certification requirement need the appropriately recognized instructor.

The Scout must earn Swimming merit badge before Scuba Diving requirements 3–6. Plan medical screening, a qualified instructor partner and policy-compliant supervision.

Official Scuba Diving requirements · Aquatics policy

Canoeing

“I have canoes and a place to paddle.”

Describe your instruction and rescue experience, not just your equipment. Ask council to assess canoe training and comparable qualifications. Plan Safety Afloat supervision, a suitable venue, life jackets, swimmer testing and rescue coverage.

The prerequisite is the Scouting America swimmer test before the subsequent Canoeing requirements. Swimming merit badge is not required for Canoeing.

Official Canoeing requirements · Counselor qualifications, GTA 7.0.1.1

05 / Put the official process to work

Send an application package, then verify approval

  1. Download and complete the official council MBC information form. Transfer your badge list and concise qualifications, or attach this supplement if accepted. Obtain the required applicant and reviewer signatures on the official form.
  2. Use the council route above and check the current membership page for the correct district contact. Submit CORI and other sensitive screening information through the council-directed process, separately from this public website.
  3. Prepare your message and attach the required completed documents yourself. The button below opens your email app; it does not send, attach files or register you.
  4. After review and any additional application steps, request confirmation of your counselor position and approved badges. Council staff maintain official registration and badge approvals; this GitHub site has no access to those systems.
Open an email draft to council membership

Before sending, add the appropriate district advancement chair when your route requires it, attach the actual forms, and edit the message to describe what you have completed. Downloading a supplement does not submit it.

Good counseling is teaching plus individual assessment

Use EDGE: Explain the purpose and safety; Demonstrate the skill; Guide practice with feedback; Enable the Scout to perform it. Your demonstration does not replace the Scout’s required demonstration. “Discuss” requires participation, and “explain” requires the Scout’s explanation. Document actual completed requirements and partials.

Follow current youth-protection rules and official FAQs, including the specific merit badge session guidance. No one-on-one adult–youth contact is allowed, including electronic communication. Plan compliant adult supervision with unit leadership; do not treat another youth as a blanket substitute for the required adult staffing.

A counselor may be eligible at 18, while particular activity-supervisor roles require adults at least 21 with additional training. Identify the counselor, instructor, activity supervisor and rescue or safety personnel separately. One credential does not cover all roles. An approved helper can provide specialized instruction where policy permits; the counselor still verifies completion.

For any activity, assess supervision, medical readiness through approved channels, weather, facilities, equipment, emergency access, permissions, accessibility, applicable laws and youth equipment restrictions. Current safety policies govern activity choices even when an older pamphlet describes a different practice. Use the authorized process for any special-needs advancement alternatives.

Canoeing

Aquatics · Aquatics qualification review

Experience to describe: Canoe instruction, paddling leadership and documented rescue competence.

Plan and verify: Equipment and waterfront access support an application but do not establish instructional competence. Plan swimmer testing, life jackets, rescue coverage and an approved water venue.

Swimmer test, not the Swimming badge: Scouts must pass the Scouting America swimmer test before the subsequent Canoeing requirements. The Swimming merit badge is not a prerequisite. For your application, describe canoe instruction, strokes, tandem and solo skills, capsize recovery, rescues and risk management—not just boat ownership. Ask the council aquatics and advancement reviewers to assess your record. For sessions, arrange Safety Afloat supervision, swimmer classification, appropriate life jackets, a safe venue, rescue coverage and weather and emergency plans.

Experience to describe: Current first aid education, emergency care, nursing, medicine or trained first aid instruction.

Care experience and CPR instructor authority are separate: Describe current first aid education, practical experience and ability to teach safe, age-appropriate skills. Pioneering

Sports & adventure · General counselor review

Experience to describe: Ropework, lashings, outdoor construction and safe project leadership.

Plan and verify: Plan sound materials, anchors, loads, tool safety and supervision; assess current height and activity limits before building anything people will use.

Knot knowledge must extend to safe projects: Describe teaching knots, splices, lashings, rope care, anchors and safe project planning. Owning rope and spars is helpful but does not establish that a bridge or tower is safe for people. The current badge expressly refers to Guide to Safe Scouting height restrictions. Check those restrictions, permitted activities, tools, loading, material condition, fall and pinch hazards and supervision before choosing a project. Obtain the counselor approval the requirement calls for before the major project; do not improvise climbing or elevated structures under the label of pioneering.

Scuba Diving

Aquatics · Recognized instructor partnership

Experience to describe: Recognized diver certification, dive experience and a plan to partner with a recognized scuba instructor.

Plan and verify: Identify the exact certification level. A diver card is not an instructor credential; arrange agency-compliant medical screening, instruction, equipment and qualified supervision.

A diver card and an instructor rating serve different roles: A PADI Open Water Diver certification is useful evidence for a counselor application; it does not authorize you to teach or certify new divers. Council must approve your counselor role and badge. The Scuba Diving pamphlet says counselors need not themselves be diving instructors, but scuba instruction must be conducted by recognized-agency instructors in good standing and approved by the local council. Partner with an appropriately qualified instructor for the Open Water certification requirement. Swimming merit badge must be earned before requirements 3–6. Record your agency, exact rating, experience and instructor partner. Check current medical screening, agency age and course rules, equipment, emergency procedures and supervision. Scuba BSA is a separate introductory award, not the Scuba Diving merit badge.

Plan and verify: Plan supervised shelter work, safe fire and water methods, weather limits and emergency access; survival exercises are not permission to leave youth unsupervised.

A survival exercise remains a supervised Scouting activity: Describe experience teaching shelter, emergency priorities, signaling, fire safety, water treatment and exposure prevention. The badge is not permission to abandon a Scout, withhold needed supplies or create a real emergency. Assess each Scout’s actual demonstrations and discussions.
